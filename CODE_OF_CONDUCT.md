# Code of Conduct

Project participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on evidence and the work.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project. Serious conduct concerns may be reported privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact options.
